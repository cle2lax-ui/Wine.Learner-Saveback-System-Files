# Caption: The Field Guide: Climate ("Climate's Impact on Wine") -- FINAL (locked with the deck, tag fg-climate-final)

**Hook (first line, under 125 characters):**
Willamette and Margaux share a latitude, not a climate: 15.9°C vs 17.7°C. Seven Factors explain why.

**Body:**
Latitude, altitude, slope, water, wind, soil and fog: seven natural Factors set a vineyard's climate. WSET sorts the result by
growing-season temperature, from cool (16.5°C or below) to hot (above 21°C). And as longer-term trends get warmer, growers are
trying new grapes, higher sites and less sunny aspects.
