Most appellations spend a century arguing for more land. Name the one currently arguing itself smaller.

Four clues on the first slide, answer on the second. It runs 50 kilometres, nearly the whole length of its region. It takes its name from a single named site at its southern end, across the river from a rather more famous hill. It was extended in 1994, and that extension took in sites that are not on hillsides.

And the debate inside it today is whether to shrink it back to the slopes.
