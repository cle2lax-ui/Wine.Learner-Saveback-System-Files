"Three Ways Into Syrah" · 1 page · Arc 1, week two

## CAPTION — paste as-is

Crozes-Hermitage surrounds the hill of Hermitage on three sides. One is mid-priced. The other is among the most expensive Syrah on earth.

The grape is the same. The climate is barely different. What changes is the ground and how steeply it sits.

Crozes runs to about 1,700 hectares on deeper, more fertile soils, at 45 hL/ha, with machine harvesting possible on the flatter land. Hermitage is 137 hectares of thin, stony, south-facing slope at 40 hL/ha, much of the work by hand, parts of it terraced against erosion. Saint-Joseph sits between them on both price and ground, running 50 kilometres and covering hillside and flat alike.

Three entry points, three budgets, one variety. Start where you like — just know what you are paying for, which is concentration, and what produces it, which is a thin soil on a steep slope that no machine can reach.
