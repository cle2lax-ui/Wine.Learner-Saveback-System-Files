# The Field Guide: Mendocino (Reel) -- voiceover script and full-length plan

## Voiceover: how it gets made
Natural, broadcast-quality voice cannot be generated in this environment (only robotic synthesizers, which would undercut
the polish). Three ways to supply it; any of them drops into the same mix:
1. **Steve records it:** a phone's voice-memo app in a quiet, soft-furnished room (a closet of clothes is ideal), phone
   15-20 cm away, slightly off-axis; one take per scene is fine. Send the files (any format).
2. **A text-to-speech service of Steve's choosing:** export WAV, one file per scene or one continuous read.
3. **Instagram's built-in text-to-speech** on text stickers, added at posting time (least control over timing).
**The mix, once a read arrives:** each line placed on its scene; the music ducked about 8 dB under the voice (sidechain, as the
kick already ducks the pad); voice leveled and de-essed; whole Reel normalized to -14 LUFS; checked against the picture events.
The picture does not change: the on-screen text already carries every fact, so the Reel also works muted (most are watched muted).

## Script for the full Reel (65 s; about 2.5-3 words a second, read warmly, unhurried)
| Time | Picture | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | The Field Guide bumper | (no voice: the sonic logo plays) |
| 3-7 s | the flag wipes; MENDOCINO | "Mendocino. California's North Coast: Anderson Valley, and its neighbors." |
| 7-15 s | California draws; the AVAs appear, 1982 to 2024 | "Twelve AVAs sit inside the county, and one more it shares with Sonoma. The famous one? Anderson Valley." |
| 15-23 s | two climates; the twist | "Near the Pacific it's cool: Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, aromatic whites. Inland it's warm, and red. But altitude flips it." |
| 23-31 s | the fog machine | "Cold air and fog pour in from the Pacific, up the Navarro River: cold evenings, cold mornings, warmer further inland." |
| 31-37 s | by the numbers | "Up to two meters of rain. Vines on the slopes. Frost in the low spots." |
| 37-45 s | the Pinot Noir | "The Pinot Noir: medium-bodied and fresh, with raspberry, cherry and plum. Good to outstanding, and priced to match." |
| 45-53 s | sparkling; the Alsace whites | "Pinot and Chardonnay make sparkling and still wines alike, and the aromatic whites follow Alsace." |
| 53-59 s | the business | "Mendocino grapes cost less than Napa's or Sonoma's, and wineries far beyond buy Anderson Valley fruit." |
| 59-65 s | the end card | "That's The Field Guide to Mendocino. Next up: guess the region." |
Every line restates on-screen facts (D3 Ch. 23.1; TTB for the AVA count; 2,000 mm is two meters). One wording check: the fog
line does not say "every evening" (D3 says cold evenings and mornings, not that fog comes nightly).
