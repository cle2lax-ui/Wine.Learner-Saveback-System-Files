Mendocino grows Alsace's grapes nine degrees south of Alsace. Here's how, in The Field Guide.

**Body:**
Twelve AVAs sit inside Mendocino County, plus one shared with Sonoma, and the famous one is Anderson Valley. Cold air
and fog pour in from the Pacific along the Navarro River, so the valley stays cool: Pinot Noir and Chardonnay for
sparkling and still wines, and the Alsace grapes too. Inland it turns warm, and red. And up on Mendocino Ridge, only
land at 1,200 feet or higher counts: islands in the sky, above the fog.
