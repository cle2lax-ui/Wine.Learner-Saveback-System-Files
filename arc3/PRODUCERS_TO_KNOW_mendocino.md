## Why the Navarro
A still, dry, Alsace-style aromatic white from a family winery in the valley -- the wine slides 8 and 9 describe
(Gewurztraminer, Riesling, Pinot Gris, Pinot Blanc; D3). The reviewer ranks Navarro's record with the grape alongside
Alsace's and Alto Adige's best.
Also considered, same source family (Wine Review Online): earlier Navarro Dry Gewurztraminers (2017, 94; 2019 "Deep
End", 94). Navarro's late-harvest Gewurztraminers score higher (97) but are dessert wines, not still dry whites.
