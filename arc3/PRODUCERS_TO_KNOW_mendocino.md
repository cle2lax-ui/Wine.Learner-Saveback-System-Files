Why these six: Roederer Estate (founded 1982 by Champagne Louis Roederer) and Domaine Anderson are valley-based; D3 names
Littorai and Williams Selyem as the valley's significant producers -- both are based in Sonoma County and buy Anderson
Valley fruit, which is D3's own point that wineries elsewhere seek it out; Black Kite and Cobb are the top scorers from
Wine Spectator's newest California Pinot Noir report.

## Not used, and why
- Scores from wine competitions (e.g. state fairs, regional challenges) and the winegrowers' association's roundups:
  they mix competition medals with critics' scores and do not always name the critic.
- Navarro Vineyards and Husch (both valley pioneers; Wine Enthusiast): no verified critic score for a current release
  found -- happy to add one if you have a bottle in mind.
- Savoy Vineyard was bought by Donum; Littorai's later bottlings from that site carry other names (e.g. "The Return",
  2023), so the slide uses the 2021.
- The list is Pinot-heavy because the valley's top critic scores are; an aromatic white (e.g. Navarro Gewurztraminer)
  could replace one Pinot if you want the page to show the Alsace-style side too.
