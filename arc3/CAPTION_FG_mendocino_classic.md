# Caption: The Field Guide: Mendocino ("The Fog Does the Work") -- DRAFT for Steve's review

**Hook (first line, under 125 characters):**
Anderson Valley sits nine degrees south of Alsace and still grows its grapes. The fog does the work.

**Body:**
Mendocino County is mostly forest: about 7,000 of its million hectares are vines. Its famous valley lies about four
miles from the Pacific, where cold air and fog funnel in along the Navarro River. The result: Pinot Noir with fresh
red fruit and medium (+) acidity, sparkling wine, and Alsace-style whites. Inland, it's warmer, and the reds change.

Have you tasted Anderson Valley Pinot Noir beside Burgundy? Which did you prefer, and why?
