A vineyard so steep it needed a staircase. Sweet, low in alcohol, loud on the nose. What's in the glass?

Read the dashboard first: sweetness, acidity, alcohol, body, aroma. Together they narrow the field faster than any single clue.

Here is the tell. Sweet at around 8% alcohol is an unusual pairing, and it usually means fermentation stopped before all the sugar became alcohol. Ask who makes wine that way, and from which grape.
