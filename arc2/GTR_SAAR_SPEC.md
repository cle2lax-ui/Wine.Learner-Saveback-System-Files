## Page 2 — reveal

**Reveal word:** THE SAAR

**Photo:** Scharzhofberg or a named Saar slope, location-verified.

**Body — four beats, matched to the four clues:**

- The Saar is a tributary of the Mosel, and the Ruwer is the other. All three sit inside the **same Anbaugebiet**.
- The best sites are in the **sheltered side valleys**, at south, south-east and south-west aspects — not on the main channel.
- **Slightly higher altitude** than the Middle Mosel, so slightly lower temperatures, and acidity in the wines that can be higher again.
- Small areas, but several highly reputed vineyards — the most famous being **Scharzhofberg**.

**Closing line:** Two rivers feed the most famous Riesling region in
the world, and neither of them is the one on the label.
