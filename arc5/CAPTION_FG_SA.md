## CAPTION: paste as-is

South Africa's vineyards sit between 32° and 35° South, cooled by two ocean currents and a wind.

Sixteen slides, from the ground up. The winelands mapped district by district, at their true shape. The cold Benguela and warm Agulhas currents and the Cape Doctor wind. Rain that falls from about 1,000 mm on the Cape Peninsula to 150 to 250 mm in the north. Three soils. And more than 370 years of history, told plainly, from 1652 to 1994.

Then the wines: Chenin Blanc in two worlds, Stellenbosch Cabernet and the Bordeaux blend, Swartland's old vines, the cool south, the Wine of Origin label, Stellenbosch's eight wards on a map, and Cap Classique, South Africa's sparkling wine made to rules.

For WSET Diploma and CSW candidates: the facts are from D3 chapter 30 (South Africa), and the Cap Classique slides from D4 chapter 20.

Which fact surprised you most? Tell us below, and watch the page for the posts that follow.
